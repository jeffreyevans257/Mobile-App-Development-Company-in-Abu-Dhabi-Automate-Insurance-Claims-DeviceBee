<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">An effective insurance application should provide useful functionality for policyholders, insurance agents, and administrators.</span></p>
<h3><strong>1. Digital Claim Submission</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Customers should be able to report accidents, property damage, medical expenses, or other insured incidents directly through their smartphones. Structured forms can collect policy numbers, incident dates, descriptions, and relevant information.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Policyholders can upload invoices, photographs, medical reports, repair estimates, and other supporting documents. OCR technology can extract relevant information from uploaded files, reducing manual data entry.</span></p>

<h3><strong>3. Real-Time Claim Tracking</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">A tracking dashboard allows customers to monitor whether their requests are submitted, under review, approved, or rejected. Automated notifications can communicate important updates and reduce unnecessary support inquiries.</span></p>
<h3><strong>4. Payment and Reimbursement Integration</strong></h3>
<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Approved claims can connect with secure payment services to simplify reimbursement procedures. Customers can also view transaction records and payment updates through their accounts.</span></p>
